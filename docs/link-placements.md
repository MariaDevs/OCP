# Link placements for onlinecasinoperu.com

## Why

onlinecasinoperu.com is DR 1.1 with 942 referring domains — the profile is spam
(Telegram link sellers, PBN shops; 19 of the top 20 domains have zero traffic and
zero dofollow links). No manual action, so Google ignores them rather than
penalising. The site ranks at positions 25–35 for anything with volume, which is
an authority problem, not a content one.

You already own better domains than anything you could realistically earn cold:

| Site | DR | Language / market | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| kasinotilmanrekisteroitymista.com | **48** | Finnish | weak topical fit |
| onlinecasinolatino.com | **27** | Spanish, LatAm | **ideal** |
| geekygambler.com | 14 | English | moderate |
| internettikasino.com | 6 | Finnish | low value |

Right now these link only from footers, which Google discounts as boilerplate
navigation rather than endorsement.

## Rules before placing anything

1. **In body text, not footers, sidebars or link lists.** A contextual sentence is
   the whole point.
2. **Vary the anchor text.** Identical anchors repeated across sites is the pattern
   Google treats as a scheme.
3. **Confirm `dofollow`.** Much of the existing spam profile is nofollow and passes
   nothing — don't repeat that by accident.
4. **Few, not many.** Three or four genuinely useful links read as normal cross-
   referencing. Twenty reads as a network and can get the lot devalued.
5. **Only where it helps the reader.** If the sentence would not exist without the
   link, don't place it.
